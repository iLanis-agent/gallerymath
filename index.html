Gallery math

A crooked row of frames starts with good intentions and ends with extra holes in the plaster. Gallery math spaces the row evenly, puts the nail at eye level on the first try, and checks whether the mixed set actually fits the wall. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Even spacing - wall, frame count and frame width become an exact gap and the first nail's offset.
  • Hang height - frame height and hanger drop become the nail height for an eye-level hang.
  • Row fit - a mixed set of small, medium and large frames checked against the wall.

Labeled norms

Eye level 145 cm from the floor. Target gap 8 cm between frames. Standard frame widths: small 20 cm, medium 30 cm, large 50 cm. Gaps and heights round to 0.1 cm. These are common hanging norms, not gallery law - your wall, your frames and your eye win.
